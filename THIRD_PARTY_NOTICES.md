## Rider–Waite–Smith card images

The files under `public/tarot/cards/` were copied from TarotWhisper's
`public/cards/` directory. TarotWhisper explicitly excludes those images from
its MIT source-code license and identifies them as scans of the 1909
Rider–Waite–Smith deck (artwork by Pamela Colman Smith), downloaded from the
Internet Archive item `rider-waite-small-jpeg`.

These images are included for development and evaluation. Confirm the terms
that apply to the specific scans in every target jurisdiction before public or
commercial redistribution. Replacing them with a separately licensed deck is
the safest production-release option.
